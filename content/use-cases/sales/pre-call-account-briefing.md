AI synthesizes 10-Ks, news, and LinkedIn data into a one-page brief before each meeting.

## What it is, how it works

Pre-call briefing assembles what a rep should know before a meeting and rarely has time to read: recent filings, news, leadership changes, product announcements, and the history of your own relationship with the account. It arrives as one page, before the call.

- Public sources and your own CRM history are synthesized together rather than listed separately.
- The brief leads with what changed since the last contact, which is usually the only genuinely new information.
- Suggested questions are derived from the gaps, not from a generic template.

## When not to use it

- A brief full of generic company background trains reps to skip it. If it doesn't say something they didn't know, it has failed.
- Public data is thin for private and small companies, and the brief will pad rather than admit that.
- Reading a fact back to a prospect without understanding it is worse than not knowing it.

## How to get started

- Generate briefs for one team for two weeks and ask which sections they actually read.
- Lead with changes since last contact and cut the standing company description entirely.
- Deliver into the calendar invite. A brief that lives in another tab does not get read.
