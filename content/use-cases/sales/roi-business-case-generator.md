AI builds a quantified value model and CFO narrative from discovery inputs, using tools like Cuvama or Vivun.

## What it is, how it works

Business case generation turns discovery inputs into a quantified value model and the narrative a CFO needs to approve spend. It builds the arithmetic the rep would otherwise assemble in a spreadsheet the night before the meeting.

- Inputs come from discovery, so the model uses the customer's own numbers rather than benchmarks.
- Assumptions are stated separately from conclusions, because assumptions are what finance will challenge.
- Sensitivity is shown, so the case survives someone disagreeing with one input.

## When not to use it

- A business case built on invented inputs is worse than no business case. If discovery did not produce real numbers, the honest move is to go back and get them.
- Overstated returns are found in the first finance review and cost you credibility for the rest of the cycle.
- Some purchases are not justified on ROI, and forcing a model onto a strategic or compliance-driven decision looks naive.

## How to get started

- Require the source of every input. A model where numbers cannot be traced will not survive procurement.
- Build the conservative case as the headline and keep the optimistic one as upside.
- Compare projected against actual for closed customers. That feedback is what makes the next model credible.
