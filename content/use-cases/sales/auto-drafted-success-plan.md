AI generates a 30/60/90 outcome plan from discovery and ROI commitments.

## What it is, how it works

Success plan drafting produces a 30, 60, and 90 day outcome plan from what the customer said they wanted and what was promised in the business case. It gives onboarding a target rather than a task list.

- Milestones derive from the ROI commitments made during the sale, so the plan measures what was sold.
- Owners are named on both sides, which is the part most plans omit.
- The draft is a starting point for the kickoff conversation rather than a document to send.

## When not to use it

- A plan built on a business case nobody believed produces milestones nobody will hit, and it damages the relationship at the first review.
- Generated plans tend toward generic milestones. If it could apply to any customer, it will not guide this one.
- Without customer agreement it is an internal document, and internal plans do not change customer behavior.

## How to get started

- Derive milestones directly from the value model rather than from a standard onboarding template.
- Review it live with the customer at kickoff and change it in the meeting.
- Check at 90 days whether the milestones were the right ones, not just whether they were met.
