An adaptive bot replaces static forms and qualifies website visitors in real time, using tools like Qualified or Drift.

## What it is, how it works

An inbound qualifier replaces the static form with a conversation. It asks what a rep would ask, routes high-intent visitors to a live conversation or a booking link, and captures the rest as structured records instead of a name and an email.

- Questions adapt to the answers, so a good-fit visitor is not asked to fill in fifteen fields.
- Qualified visitors can be routed straight to a calendar while intent is high.
- Everything captured lands in the CRM as fields, not as a chat log nobody reads.

## When not to use it

- A bot that cannot answer product questions frustrates exactly the high-intent visitors you most want to keep.
- Hiding documentation or pricing behind a chat interface pushes technical buyers to a competitor rather than into a conversation.
- Without live coverage during your buyers' working hours, the handoff to a human breaks at the moment it matters.

## How to get started

- Deploy on high-intent pages first, such as pricing and comparison, rather than sitewide.
- Give it a fast, visible route to a human. That single option determines whether people engage.
- Measure meetings booked, not conversations started.
