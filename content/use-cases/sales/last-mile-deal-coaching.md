AI surfaces deal-specific risk and a recommended next step for the AE and manager, using tools like Aviso or Clari Copilot.

## What it is, how it works

Last-mile coaching looks at a specific late-stage deal and recommends the next action, surfacing the risk that most threatens it. It's narrower than pipeline review: one deal, one recommendation, aimed at the AE and their manager together.

- Recommendations are specific to the deal rather than generic best practice.
- Risk is named explicitly, such as a missing economic buyer or a stalled legal review.
- Manager and rep see the same view, which is what makes the conversation productive.

## When not to use it

- Advice that generalizes across deals reads as filler and gets ignored after a week.
- If it only fires in the final two weeks, it's a report on a decided outcome rather than coaching.
- Used to check up on reps rather than help them, it changes what gets logged rather than what gets done.

## How to get started

- Restrict to deals above a value threshold in the current quarter, where attention is worth spending.
- Bring it into existing deal reviews rather than creating a separate ritual.
- Track whether recommended actions were taken and whether those deals closed.
