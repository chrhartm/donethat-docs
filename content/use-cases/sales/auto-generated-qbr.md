AI synthesizes usage, value delivered, and expansion vectors into a business-review deck, using tools like Gainsight Horizon AI.

## What it is, how it works

QBR generation assembles the business review from usage data, support history, and the outcomes promised during the sale, producing a deck that argues value delivered rather than features shipped.

- Content is built around the customer's stated goals rather than a standard agenda.
- Value delivered is quantified where the data supports it and stated qualitatively where it does not.
- Expansion opportunities are identified from usage patterns rather than from quota pressure.

## When not to use it

- A QBR that claims value the customer does not recognize damages the relationship more than skipping the meeting.
- Where usage data is thin, generated decks pad with charts nobody asked for and the customer notices.
- If the honest answer is that value was not delivered, a generated deck will not say so, and the CSM must.

## How to get started

- Anchor every deck to the milestones from the original success plan. That comparison is the whole point.
- Have the CSM cut at least one slide before every review. Generated completeness is not the goal.
- Ask the customer which section was useful and drop the rest.
