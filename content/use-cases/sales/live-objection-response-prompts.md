AI surfaces real-time rebuttals during pricing and competitive pushback on calls, using tools like Cresta or Balto.

## What it is, how it works

Objection prompting listens for pricing and competitive pushback during a live call and surfaces an approved rebuttal within seconds. It targets the moments where reps most often improvise badly, and where improvising badly is expensive.

- Detection is narrow by design, focused on pricing and named competitors rather than every objection.
- Rebuttals come from approved battle cards, not from generation on the fly.
- Prompts are private to the rep and disappear when the moment passes.

## When not to use it

- Reading a scripted rebuttal is audible to the buyer, and a stilted response damages the conversation more than an imperfect improvised one.
- Latency requirements are severe here. A rebuttal arriving twenty seconds late is worse than useless.
- Competitive claims carry legal exposure. Prompts that make comparative assertions need the same review as published material.

## How to get started

- Limit to your three most common objections. Coverage breadth is not the win.
- Rehearse the rebuttals offline first through [negotiation role-play simulator](./negotiation-role-play-simulator.md), so reps internalize rather than read them.
- Review recorded calls where prompts fired to check whether they helped or interrupted.

## Best practices / further reading

- Broader in-call assistance is [real-time call coaching](./real-time-call-coaching.md), with the same cognitive load caveat.
- Real-time assist tools include [Cresta](https://www.cresta.com/) and [Balto](https://balto.ai/).
