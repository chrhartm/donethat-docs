AI generates tailored video walkthroughs per prospect using an avatar or product clips, with tools like Tavus or HeyGen.

## What it is, how it works

Personalized demo video generates a short walkthrough tailored to one prospect, using an avatar or stitched product clips, addressing their stated use case rather than the standard tour. It fills the gap between a live demo and a generic recording.

- Scripts are generated from discovery notes and the prospect's stated priorities.
- Product clips are assembled per use case rather than recorded fresh each time.
- Delivery is asynchronous, which suits buying committees who never attend the live call.

## When not to use it

- Avatar video sits in an uncanny middle ground for many buyers. A plain screen recording with a real voice often outperforms it.
- Production effort per prospect only pays back on large deals. For transactional sales it is not worth the setup.
- Disclosure matters. Presenting a synthetic presenter as a real person damages trust when discovered, and it usually is.

## How to get started

- Test against a plain human-recorded Loom on similar deals before investing in avatars.
- Target the committee members who never join calls, which is where async video genuinely helps.
- Keep it under three minutes. Completion rate falls off sharply past that.
