An AI buyer bot lets reps rehearse tough objections before live calls, using tools like Hyperbound or Second Nature.

## What it is, how it works

A role-play simulator gives reps an AI buyer to practice against. It pushes back on price, raises competitive comparisons, and stalls the way real buyers do, so the first time a rep hears a hard objection is not on a live call.

- The buyer persona is configured to your market and common objections.
- Sessions are repeatable, so a rep can run the same scenario until the response is fluent.
- Feedback comes after the session rather than during it, which is the opposite of live coaching.

## When not to use it

- Simulated buyers are more predictable than real ones, and over-practice produces reps who handle the simulation well and get surprised by people.
- Mandated practice hours turn it into compliance theater. Voluntary use tracks actual value.
- The persona reflects whoever configured it, including their blind spots.

## How to get started

- Build personas from real lost-deal objections rather than from imagination.
- Use it for onboarding and for new product launches, where the gap between knowledge and fluency is widest.
- Let reps choose scenarios. Self-directed practice gets used.
