An LLM drafts cold openers grounded in a prospect's recent posts, podcasts, or filings, using tools like Lavender or Smartwriter.

## What it is, how it works

Personalized drafting writes a cold opener grounded in something specific about the prospect: a recent post, a podcast appearance, a filing, a product launch. The rep gets a draft with the reference already found, which is the part that actually takes time.

- Research and drafting happen together, so the personalization is real rather than a merge field.
- Tone and structure follow your existing sequences rather than a generic template.
- The rep edits before sending, which is where the judgment stays.

## When not to use it

- Personalization at volume stops being personalization. Prospects recognize the pattern quickly, and a well-researched opener sent to ten thousand people is still spam.
- Getting the reference slightly wrong is worse than sending nothing personal. A misattributed quote ends the conversation.
- Regulated industries and formal buying cultures often respond badly to the casual register these tools default to.

## How to get started

- Have reps rate the first fifty drafts before any go out. The bad ones share a pattern you can correct.
- Keep a human send step. Automated send at volume is what gets domains blocked.
- Compare reply rates against your existing sequence rather than against zero.
