AI detects when the primary contact leaves the account and triggers a retention play, using tools like Champify or UserGems.

## What it is, how it works

Departure monitoring watches for your main contact leaving the account. Losing a champion is one of the clearest renewal risks there is, and it is often discovered weeks later when an email bounces.

- Job-change data and email signals detect the move, usually before anyone tells you.
- The alert names the departed contact, their role in the account, and what they sponsored.
- The same signal is an opportunity, since the champion has landed somewhere new.

## When not to use it

- Job-change data is noisy, and a wrong alert that prompts a clumsy outreach is embarrassing.
- Tracking individuals across employers has privacy implications that differ by region.
- An alert with no play attached is just news. The value is in what happens next.

## How to get started

- Monitor sponsors and economic buyers only, not every contact.
- Define both plays in advance: retention at the old account, outreach at the new one.
- Confirm the departure before acting. A single data source is not enough to act on.

- Relationship coverage that reduces this exposure is measured by [buying committee mapping](./buying-committee-mapping.md).
