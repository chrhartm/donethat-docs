An end-to-end agent runs prospecting, enrichment, outreach, and meeting booking without rep involvement, using tools like Artisan Ava or 11x.

## What it is, how it works

An autonomous BDR agent runs the whole top of funnel: building the list, enriching it, writing and sending outreach, handling replies, and booking meetings. It's the most complete automation in the prospecting stage and the one that most changes what the role is.

- The agent owns the sequence end to end, including reply handling and follow-up timing.
- Meetings land in a rep's calendar with the conversation history attached.
- Guardrails on volume, targeting, and messaging are configuration, not code.

## When not to use it

- Your domain reputation is at stake, and a misconfigured agent can damage it in days in a way that takes months to repair.
- Buyers increasingly recognize agent-written outreach, and the market's tolerance is falling faster than the tools are improving.
- If nobody owns the output, quality drifts quietly. This needs more management attention than a human BDR, not less, at least at first.

## How to get started

- Run it on a segment you're not currently covering. Testing on your best accounts risks the relationships that matter.
- Use a separate sending domain until deliverability is proven.
- Judge it on meetings held and pipeline created, never on emails sent or replies received.
