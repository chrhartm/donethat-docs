AI synthesizes spend data and market signals to surface consolidation, renegotiation, and switch opportunities across categories on a rolling basis.

## What it is, how it works

This reads spend data and market signals together. It surfaces where consolidation, renegotiation, or a switch would pay. It runs all year instead of waiting for the annual review, so findings arrive while you can still act on them.

- Fragmentation, price drift against benchmark, and expiring contracts are the signals that generate most findings.
- Each opportunity carries an estimated value and the evidence behind it.
- Findings are ranked, because a category team can pursue a few things well or many badly.

## When not to use it

- An estimate is an estimate. Publish a pipeline of unchecked savings and procurement spends the year defending it.
- Without category manager capacity, an opportunity queue is just a longer to-do list.
- Market signals are thin in niche categories. The model leans on spend data alone and won't tell you it did.

## How to get started

- Validate the top five findings by hand before trusting the ranking. That calibrates the value estimates.
- Match the queue length to the number of sourcing events your team can actually run in a quarter.
- Track which opportunities converted, and use that to reweight the ranking.
