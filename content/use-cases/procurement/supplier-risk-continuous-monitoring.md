AI monitors news, financial filings, and ESG events for active suppliers and triggers alerts when risk thresholds are breached, using tools like Coupa Risk or Riskmethods.

## What it is, how it works

Continuous monitoring watches your active supplier base for financial distress, adverse media, sanctions changes, and ESG events, and raises an alert when a defined threshold is crossed. Risk assessment stops being an annual exercise and becomes an ongoing one.

- Coverage spans filings, news, sanctions and watchlists, and commercial risk data.
- Alerts are scoped to suppliers that matter, since monitoring everything produces noise proportional to your supplier count.
- Each alert carries the source, so the first question in the response is already answered.

## When not to use it

- Entity matching is the hard part. Common company names generate false positives that erode trust fast.
- Alerts without an agreed response plan create anxiety rather than resilience. Decide in advance who acts and how.
- Monitoring tier one only gives false comfort where the real fragility sits deeper in the chain.

## How to get started

- Scope to critical suppliers first, defined by disruption impact rather than by spend.
- Write the response playbook before enabling alerts. The first genuine alert is the wrong time to invent a process.
- Tune thresholds for a month with alerts going to one person, then widen distribution.
