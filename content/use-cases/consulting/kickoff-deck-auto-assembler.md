LLM generates a kickoff presentation from SOW, team bios, client context, and a standard agenda template.

## What it is, how it works

Deck assembly builds the kickoff presentation from the SOW, team bios, client context, and your standard agenda. Kickoff decks are largely assembly work, and assembly is what this does well.

- Scope and deliverable slides are generated from the SOW so the deck matches the contract.
- Team slides pull current bios rather than whatever was in the last deck.
- The output is an editable draft in your own template.

## When not to use it

- A kickoff deck sets the tone of the relationship. One that reads as assembled tells the client how much attention they are getting.
- Scope slides generated from a SOW with unresolved ambiguity will present that ambiguity as settled.
- The value is in the conversation the deck supports, and generating slides does not prepare anyone for it.

## How to get started

- Generate the scope and team sections only, and write the framing slides by hand.
- Check the scope slides against the SOW line by line before the meeting.
- Cut at least a third of the generated deck. Completeness is not the goal at kickoff.
