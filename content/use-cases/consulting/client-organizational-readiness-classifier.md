LLM assesses change capacity, capability gaps, and political feasibility for each recommendation before it is presented.

## What it is, how it works

This rates each recommendation against what the client can actually do. It looks at appetite for change, skills gaps, competing projects, and whether the politics allow it. A recommendation the client cannot act on will not happen.

- The read comes from interview notes and what the team saw on the ground.
- Politics is reported apart from skills. The fix for each is different.
- A low score changes the phasing. It does not drop the idea.

## When not to use it

- Judging politics from interviews is a guess. Getting it wrong in writing is awkward once the document travels.
- Readiness can become an excuse for timid advice. That is its own kind of failure.
- The client knows their own shop better than the model does. Use it to start a conversation.

## How to get started

- Keep the political assessment in a separate internal document with restricted circulation.
- Use it to change sequencing and support, not to soften the recommendation.
- Test the assessment with the client sponsor privately before it shapes the final report.
