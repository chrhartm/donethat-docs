LLM generates structured behavioral and technical interview questions from a project role description and required competencies.

## What it is, how it works

This builds a structured interview from a role and its required skills. Questions are tied to what the job needs, both behavioral and technical. It replaces the improvised interview that changes with whoever runs it.

- Questions map to named competencies rather than general capability.
- Follow-up probes are generated for each question, which is where interviews usually go shallow.
- The same question set across candidates is what makes comparison fair.

## When not to use it

- Generated questions inherit bias from the role description. Review them first, especially anything touching background or culture.
- Interviewers reading questions mechanically conduct worse interviews than those who improvise well.
- Hiring rules differ by country. Consistency is often a legal duty, not a preference.

## How to get started

- Have a hiring lead review and cut the set before first use.
- Keep the competency list short. Six questions asked well beats twenty asked quickly.
- Compare candidate scores against later delivery performance to see whether the competencies were the right ones.
