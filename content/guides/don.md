Don is DoneThat's proactive agent. He can help you stay on track, coach you, and cheer you on.

Everybody has a different need for interaction with Don, though. That's why you can specify in your [settings](/settings) which actions Don should be allowed to take and specify custom prompts. Don can also adjust those when you ask him to.
